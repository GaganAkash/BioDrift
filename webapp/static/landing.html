OS-level evidence

Every package runs in an isolated subprocess under sys.audit hooks. Real files, network calls, spawned processes and imports are observed — nothing is guessed or inferred.

Contracts.

A written rulebook per package: what it may read, write, connect to, and run. The audit trail is checked against the rulebook, not against a fuzzy profile.

Honest verdicts

COMPLIANT, VIOLATION, or INCONCLUSIVE — including coverage of what was and wasn't observed, so "we didn't look long enough" is never hidden.
